Chapter 1: 13 December Ki Roshni

Meri pyaari Bebo, aaj ka din meri zindagi ke sabse khoobsurat dinon mein se ek hai, kyunki aaj ke din aap is duniya mein aayin. Aap sirf meri mohabbat nahi, balki meri har dua ka sabse khoobsurat jawab hain. Allah aapko hamesha khush, salamat aur muskurata rakhe.

Chapter 2: 13 Aur 17 December Ka Rishta

Dekhein na, Allah ne hamari birthdays mein bhi sirf 4 din ka faasla rakha hai—13 December ko aap aur 17 December ko main. Jaise hamari tareekhein ek doosre ke bilkul qareeb hain, waise hi hamare dil bhi hamesha ek doosre ke saath jude rahenge.

Chapter 3: Aapki Izzat Aur Aapka Saath

Main aapse wada karta hoon ke zindagi ke har mod par main aapki izzat, aapki khushi aur aapke bharose ko sabse upar rakhunga. Chahe waqt kaisa bhi ho, aapka Hani hamesha aapke saath dhaal ban kar khada rahega.

Chapter 4: Aane Wale Saalon Ka Wada

Yeh sirf ek birthday nahi, balki hamare aane wale har saal ki khushiyon ki shuruat hai. Dua hai ke agle har saal hum isi tarah ek doosre ki khushiyan manate rahein. Happy Birthday once again, meri jaan! ❤️
